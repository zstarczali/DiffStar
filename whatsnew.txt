DiffStar - What's new
=====================

0.2.1
-----
- Fix: the duplicate search could not always be stopped. It shared its
  stop flag with the folder comparison, so a comparison starting in
  another tab (or a stop sent before the search had really started,
  e.g. while a sleeping disk spun up) swallowed the Stop. Every search
  now has its own stop flag and file counter.
- Duplicate search: Esc stops it too, the button shows "Stopping…"
  at once, and closing the tab stops a running search.
- Fix: the Windows-1250 / Windows-1252 encoding names in Settings stayed
  Hungarian in the English interface.

0.2.0
-----
- Folder comparison: three views, as in FtpStar: Details (tree), Small
  icons and Large icons. The icon views show one folder at a time with a
  breadcrumb bar; every tile holds the left and the right picture side by
  side (image previews), coloured by status. Folders open with a double
  click, Backspace goes up. The chosen view is remembered.
- Duplicate finder (toolbar, folder view, Ctrl+Shift+D): files with the
  same content in one or two folders, also inside one folder. Files are
  grouped by size, then by a hash of their beginning, then by a full
  SHA-256. Groups are listed by reclaimable space, with image previews
  (list or large cards), a minimum size and an "only between the two
  folders" filter.
- Quick selection for duplicates: keep the oldest / newest / shortest
  path in every group, or select the copies of one side. Selected files
  go to the recycle bin (restorable); a group can never lose all copies.
- Fix: the folder options bar wrapped the mode selector onto two lines
  and pushed the filters out of the window.

0.1.0
-----
- First version: text, folder, image and binary (hex) comparison, merging
  and editing, AI explanation with advice and follow-up questions, git
  difftool integration, themes from GitStar.
